How it works

Two friends, Sam and Lucía, talk about something real: a balloon flight, a night shift on a cargo ramp, a client meeting. Early stages are almost all in your language. Each stage swaps in more of the other language, and by the last stage the conversation is entirely in it. You meet each word in context several times, with a pronunciation guide that fades after a few uses.

The pronunciation guides are a bridge, not the real sound. Listen to the audio, which uses your device's own speech voices.
