El globo de Teseo

An old puzzle about identity, retold with a balloon, and a famous line from Ortega y Gasset.

How to study this page
  1. Press Play this stage before you read. Listen first hides the text until you've heard it.
  2. Read along. Tap any word you don't know to hear it and see what it means.
  3. When a stage opens with Words ahead, go through those words first. They are new in that stage.
  4. Press Repeat after me: the page reads each line, then pauses so you can say it out loud.
  5. Answer the question under the stage, then try Check yourself.
  6. On the last stage, do Your turn: say the line in Spanish before you reveal the answer.
  7. The next day, replay the last stage with guides off.
Guides Speed Voices

Original dialogue. The Ship of Theseus puzzle comes from Plutarch's Life of Theseus; the twist of rebuilding a second ship from the old planks was added by Thomas Hobbes. "Yo soy yo y mi circunstancia" is from José Ortega y Gasset's Meditaciones del Quijote (1914). The full sentence continues: "y si no la salvo a ella no me salvo yo." Spanish follows Latin American pronunciation. Audio uses your device's speech voices.
